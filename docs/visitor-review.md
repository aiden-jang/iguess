# Visitor walkthrough

Use two people and disposable local rooms. This guide describes an observed session;
it does not claim one has happened. Keep private drawings, names and reconnect credentials
out of recordings or public reports.

1. Ask the first person to start a Classic room and invite the second. Observe whether
   fresh entry, invitations, rejoining and the need for another person are understood.
2. Let the host change a rule. Ask the guest to explain who can start the game and
   what Ready means. Watch invalid custom words and save feedback.
3. Let the host choose a word and draw. Confirm the guesser sees only a masked hint.
   Observe whether the drawing tools and guess field are easy to find on each device.
4. Have the guesser answer correctly. Watch whether success, scores, role changes and
   the next turn are understandable without coaching.
5. Interrupt a connection while someone has a draft. Observe their recovery and retry;
   confirm the draft and room identity survive without duplicate sends.
6. Finish the game. Ask them to replay a drawing, save the evidence and share the
   prepared image. Cancel one share sheet and check that the result feels safe.
7. Try a failed invitation copy/share, then ask them to recover from the selectable link.
   Check keyboard access, reduced motion, enlarged text and both palettes.

Record the revision, device/browser, completed task, hesitation, unexpected result and recovery.
Separate what each person discovered from explanations you supplied. Prioritize lost work,
blocked entry and misunderstood permissions ahead of cosmetic preferences.
